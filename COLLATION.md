# Collation coverage and unresolved readings

AI initial transcription: 140/140 IIIF canvases. Independent AI source-first reading, full segment comparison and source adjudication integrated for all 140/140 canvases (c001–c140). Human/expert review: not-run. Formal validation does not certify character-level correctness.

The 140-canvas coverage records independent source-first readings followed by comparison and adjudication. Source layout, blank areas, page furniture and small paired lines were included. Matching high-risk readings were also questioned against the source. This is AI editorial work, not human double collation, palaeographic certification or peer review.

Original page/unit/segment and UNC/REV identifiers are preserved. Positions are 1-based Unicode code points within the named segment; no normalization or traditional/simplified conversion is performed. Paired small lines retain right-then-left order. Original readings, historical alternatives and current decisions remain distinguishable. Source observations retain glyph reasons, counterevidence, source hashes and recorded regions, while local image files and operational record pointers are excluded.

An unknown symbol has only the extent declared in its UNC record. `extent=1` describes one identifiable source position, not certainty about the glyph. `extent=unknown` stands for a source cluster whose length cannot be established. Candidate lists are possibilities, not replacements. Counts of □ positions, UNC records and historical missing characters are not interchangeable. Do not reconstruct numbers arithmetically or expand clipped titles/folios from expectation.

Human/expert review is not-run. Modern punctuation remains an unreviewed editorial layer limited to listed units on c005–c021. Schema, checksum and round-trip checks verify encoding and internal consistency, not source correctness.

Historical limitations are retained. Annotation frames on c001/c002 differ from native JPEG frames and lack a verified mapping. Some older numeric regions lack a confirmed xywh/xyxy interpretation. Original p069 segment IDs map textually to canvas c071. For c065–c068, sixteen early proposal files were revised without retaining their preceding bytes; old hashes cannot reconstruct those missing versions, and reconstruction is not claimed. Frozen independent readings, the initial transcription baseline and source images were unaffected. This limits reconstruction of that early proposal history; it does not erase the final accepted source-comparison coverage.

The initial-reader observations retained under `source_observations` are labelled as such and are not counted as additional independent readings. Prior source observations embedded in UNC/REV describe their historical editorial state; the named current segment and active UNC govern the current reading.
